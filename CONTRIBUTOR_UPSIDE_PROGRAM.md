# Contributor Upside Program

This document describes a potential SeemoreCodez contributor recognition and upside program for Philadelphia Drive. It is not part of the MIT License.

This document is not a public securities offering, not an employment offer, not a partnership offer, not a guarantee of payment, and not legal, tax, investment, or financial advice. No rights exist unless SeemoreCodez and the contributor sign a separate written agreement.

## Purpose

SeemoreCodez may choose to reward the three contributors who create the greatest verified impact for the project through:

- Technical advancement
- Adoption, traffic, attention, and ecosystem growth
- Documentation, support, education, and institutional enablement
- Security, reliability, accessibility, and production readiness work

## Potential Award

Subject to a separate signed agreement, up to three eligible contributors may each receive:

- Name recognition in official project materials, such as `AUTHORS.md`, `NOTICE.md`, release notes, or an official contributor page.
- A potential 2% share of a defined contributor sale-proceeds pool if SeemoreCodez sells the project, the official commercial offering, or a qualifying project-related business asset.

The phrase "2% share" must be defined in the separate written agreement before it has any effect. The agreement should define whether the share applies to gross proceeds, net proceeds, founder proceeds, asset-sale proceeds, equity-sale proceeds, or another specifically defined pool.

## No Automatic Rights

No person receives ownership, equity, securities, revenue share, compensation, sale proceeds, voting rights, governance rights, or license rights merely by:

- Opening issues
- Creating pull requests
- Writing documentation
- Promoting the project
- Sending traffic
- Building integrations
- Participating in discussion
- Having contributions accepted
- Being listed in `AUTHORS.md`

Rights exist only if a separate written agreement is signed by SeemoreCodez and the contributor.

## Eligibility

Eligibility may consider:

- Accepted technical contributions
- Measurable product advancement
- Verified non-bot traffic or adoption impact
- Institutional adoption support
- Security, accessibility, and reliability improvements
- High-quality documentation, examples, and education
- Community support and issue triage
- Reputation-building attention that materially benefits the official project

SeemoreCodez may exclude spam, bot traffic, paid traffic without approval, misleading promotion, low-quality activity, harmful conduct, license violations, security violations, policy violations, or activity that creates legal, reputational, or institutional risk.

## Suggested Impact Weights

Unless a separate agreement uses different weights, SeemoreCodez may evaluate impact using this guide:

- 50% product and technical advancement
- 25% verified adoption, traffic, attention, and ecosystem growth
- 15% documentation, support, education, and institutional enablement
- 10% security, reliability, accessibility, and maintainability

These weights are guidance only and do not create binding rights.

## Selection Authority

SeemoreCodez has final authority to decide whether the program is active, who is eligible, what measurement period applies, what evidence is accepted, and whether any separate agreement will be offered.

Before offering binding contributor upside, SeemoreCodez should obtain legal and tax review.
