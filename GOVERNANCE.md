# Governance

Philadelphia Drive is a founder-led open-source project maintained by SeemoreCodez.

## Founder Steward

Frank/SeemoreCodez is the founder steward and final decision-maker for:

- Project vision and roadmap
- Official releases
- Package publishing
- Security posture
- Maintainer access
- Repository settings
- Documentation site
- Domains and official channels
- Institutional relationships
- Commercial offerings
- Private or hosted offerings
- Trademark and brand use

## Maintainers

Maintainers may be invited to help review issues, triage pull requests, manage releases, improve documentation, or support the community.

Maintainer permissions are delegated, limited, and revocable. Maintainer status does not grant ownership, equity, trademark rights, package ownership, commercial authority, or authority to bind SeemoreCodez.

## Decision Model

The project welcomes community input and contribution. Final decisions remain with the founder steward.

SeemoreCodez may decline or remove work that creates security risk, legal uncertainty, brand confusion, institutional risk, roadmap drift, maintenance burden, privacy risk, or commercial conflict.

## Commercial Layer

The public core may remain MIT open source while SeemoreCodez sells official services and commercial offerings, including:

- Institutional onboarding
- Campus policy packs
- Private adapters
- Hosted or self-hosted hubs
- Support subscriptions
- SLAs
- Training
- Certification
- Procurement and security review support
- Custom integrations

## Changes To Governance

SeemoreCodez may update this governance model as the project matures. Material changes should be documented in the repository.
