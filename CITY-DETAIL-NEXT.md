# Philadelphia detail: verified sources and next steps

Checked September 11, 2026. The practical free upgrade is **mapped sidewalk/road boundaries now, followed by a bounded reconstruction of measured roofs and bridge decks from 2022 LiDAR**. The source review did not verify a newer, freely reusable, citywide textured Philadelphia mesh. These datasets do not establish complete AAA street detail, present-day facade textures, interiors, or destructible building construction.

## Actual sidewalk, road and bridge boundaries

[Philadelphia Impervious Surfaces 2015, PASDA layer 11](https://mapservices.pasda.psu.edu/server/rest/services/pasda/CityPhilly/MapServer/11) provides polygon geometry, including holes. Its [source metadata](https://www.pasda.psu.edu/uci/FullMetadataDisplay.aspx?file=PhiladelphiaImperviousSurfaces2015.xml) identifies spring 2015 leaf-off 3-inch orthophotography, with feature/attribute updates in October 2016. This is plan geometry, not a measured curb-height or bridge-deck mesh.

Useful `FCODE` values: `1000` travelway, `1010` median, `1200` travelway bridge, `1210` railroad bridge, `1220` pedestrian bridge, `2210` sidewalk, `2230` driveway, `2240` alley. The hosted API fields are `OBJECTID`, `OBJECTID_1`, `FCODE`, `Shape_Length`, `Shape_Area`; older source metadata lists more fields than this service actually exposes. Use `OBJECTID_1` as its query object identifier and request `outSR=4326`. Returned ring coordinates in the verified responses are 2D. Do not infer surveyed width, elevation or slab material from unavailable attributes.

The [City catalog](https://opendataphilly.org/datasets/impervious-surfaces/) identifies the **City of Philadelphia License** and **Public Use; Free**. It retains City rights and supplies the data as-is. The service's `copyrightText` is **Pictometry Intelligent Images, Intergraph Services Company (ISC), and Sanborn Mapping Services**. Preserve that source credit, with City of Philadelphia identified as the data source and PASDA as the distributor. This is the project's provenance policy based on the published labels; no additional prescribed credit-line format was established in this review. The app's MIT code license does not relicense the source data.

Direct, credential-free query:

`https://mapservices.pasda.psu.edu/server/rest/services/pasda/CityPhilly/MapServer/11/query`

Use `where=FCODE=2210`, a bounded envelope, `inSR=4326`, `outSR=4326`, `returnGeometry=true`, and `outFields=OBJECTID,OBJECTID_1,FCODE,Shape_Area`. An actual 140 m envelope around `[-75.16469,39.94746]` returned **33 features, 57 rings including 24 holes, 2,657 vertices, 112,980 response bytes**, HTTP 200 with an allowed localhost browser origin. No API key or account was used. Evidence: `artifacts/city-detail/spawn-sidewalks.json` and its adjacent headers. A separate Fishtown query also returned actual sidewalks: IDs `790178`, `797327`, `797842` in `[-75.136,39.9695,-75.128,39.9755]`; those large polygons extend beyond the query envelope, so geometry must be clipped locally rather than rendered wholesale.

`src/sidewalk-surfaces.js` implements nearby source boundaries with holes retained, terrain sampled per vertex, and stable 6 m damage sections. The 8 cm thickness, plain concrete colour, and damage divisions are explicit display/gameplay choices. The data does not describe those details. The layer has at most 96 retained sections within its nearby query, displays within 90 m, and never blocks the car. `destruction.js` can animate small fragments cut from a removed section, capped at 12 per break and 24 overall, settling and fading over 2.4 seconds. This neither excavates terrain nor erases the sidewalk in the source aerial image. It is a ground-detail/gameplay improvement, not a new road-height survey or facade upgrade. Browser visual verification remains an integration step.

## Measured roof and bridge geometry: next bounded pilot

[NOAA's 2022 Philadelphia LiDAR record](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/70168), updated March 2026, describes approximately 239 square miles, QL1 capture with at least 8 pulses/m² and 16 pulses/m² in the city center. The collection's detailed time reference is March 29, 2022; its abstract says April 2022. Use “spring 2022” when that distinction is immaterial. Access constraints are none; direct downloads need no account or key. Credit **City of Philadelphia Office of Innovation and Technology, Merrick, NOAA Office for Coastal Management, PASDA**. Historical conditions and the published use limitations still apply.

The [public NOAA download index](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/laz/geoid18/9848/index.html) lists **752 COPC LAZ files and a full download size of 95G**. It provides a tile index and compressed point data. Its class summary includes **46,186,943 bridge-deck points (class 17)**. It has no class-6 building population; most nonground data remains class 1. Roof extraction therefore needs filtering against real footprints plus geometric segmentation, not a nonexistent “building points” switch. Bare-earth DEM alone must not be used to flatten elevated road decks.

The small [EPT manifest](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/entwine/geoid18/9848/ept.json) was read directly without credentials. It reports **17,144,434,854 points**, LASzip nodes, and no Red/Green/Blue fields. Its coordinate system is **NAD83(2011) / UTM 18N (EPSG:6347), NAVD88 height in metres**. That explicit manifest supersedes a generic geographic-coordinate description elsewhere in the catalog. Transform the horizontal coordinates and vertical datum before combining with Cesium ellipsoid heights. Class filtering must remove noise; even the manifest's raw Z range contains outliers. These are uncolored measured points, not photogrammetry or a ready-to-drive mesh.

For scale context, the [PASDA 2022 products directory](https://www.pasda.psu.edu/download/phillyLiDAR/2022/Products/) lists a 3-foot DEM ZIP at **2,407,571,028 bytes**, its hillshade at **568,673,695 bytes**, and hydro/contour breaklines at **3,376,096,867 bytes**. The 3-foot DEM is much finer than the current broad terrain, but does not itself provide roof surfaces. No whole-city point files or product ZIPs were downloaded for this review.

The suggested next pilot is the Fishtown box above, immediately beyond part of the existing downtown model extent. Request only its EPT/COPC subset; record actual bytes and coordinate transformations; separate bridge decks and measured roof candidates; reconstruct only supported surfaces; compare the result against source points. Package a local 3D Tiles/mesh pilot for Cesium. Its production effort and download size are **not yet measured**. Frank's clarification requires preserving photographic detail while selectively removing baked objects. The original aerial imagery is restored as the baseline until a faithful replacement is available. It does not supply missing street-facing photos.

## Ground-photo removal

The plain-ground experiment used styled exports of actual PASDA surface/hydrography polygons. Frank rejected that visual replacement because it removed useful photographed detail along with the unwanted objects. It is no longer imported by the game. Preserve those boundaries as supporting geometry data; do not promote plain colors as the requested realistic city. GROUND-CLEANUP.md preserves the rejected experiment, and RECONSTRUCTION-TARGET.md states the corrected requirement. Selective reconstruction and cleanup remain unfinished.

## Fidelity limit

Current I3S coverage is roughly `[-75.190418721,39.940180742,-75.133563703,39.969870911]`, from the existing mixed textured/untextured downtown dataset. Public footprint extrusions beyond it give approximate massing. LiDAR can improve actual roof shape, elevation changes and some bridge decks, but cannot recover unseen facades, trim, shopfronts, signs, surface materials or interiors. Reaching the requested full-city AAA appearance would additionally require suitable licensed facade capture and substantial authored asset work, plus streaming and performance validation. A procedural texture or generic replacement building must not be presented as an observed Philadelphia exterior.
